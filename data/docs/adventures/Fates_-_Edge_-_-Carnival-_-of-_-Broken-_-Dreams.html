🎪 The Carnival of Broken Dreams

Tier I–III 3–6 Sessions Folk Horror Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

A traveling night-market of tents, music, and miracles glides the trade-roads between Silkstrand and Thepyrgos. It grants wishes—precisely—and charges a price in things people didn't know they valued. As the Desire Index rises, a town's intangibles unravel: lullabies vanish, bell-rights lapse, markets drift off schedule. The party can expose terms, retune the engine, or divert its appetite before the carnival moves on.

Reading an encounter badge. Each badge names a DV and a Position. It does not name a dice pool, and that is deliberate: the pool is Attribute + Skill, chosen from how the player says they are going about it. Two characters can attempt the same encounter with different pools and both be right. Where an approach is obviously the intended one, the badge says so; where it does not, ask what they are doing and build the pool from the answer.

🔥 Act I — Firelight

Tier I 1–2 Sessions

The carnival parade arrives; a "free wish" hooks an important NPC. The party sees a miracle, then discovers the missing price. Gain your first Diamond through a fair-game contest or etiquette duel.

🎪 The Firelight Parade
Lanterns, brass, thrilled hush. The carnival arrives with a parade of wonders. A free raffle-wish is "won" by a sympathetic NPC. Staff "clarifies" nothing. The crowd is entranced. Something is wrong.
Timer: Desire Index [6] Timer: Community Unraveling [6] Encounter: The Raffle Wish (DV 3, Controlled)
📜 The Witness Seal
To expose the carnival's terms, you must win a Witness Seal — a Diamond that compels staff to name the exact price aloud. Compete in a fair-game or etiquette duel to earn it.
Timer: Spotlight Heat [4] Encounter: The Fair-Game Contest (DV 4, Controlled)
💎 Witness Seal — Compels staff to name the exact price aloud
🌙 Firelight Fallout
The first wish has been granted. The price is being collected. The community is beginning to fray. The party must decide how to proceed.
Timer: Carnival Appetite [8]

🪞 Act II — Mirrors & Ledgers

Tier II 1–3 Sessions

Investigate the carnival's tents, negotiate terms, steal leverage from the Ledger Wagon, and win Diamonds to constrain predation.

🏛️ House of Honest Mirrors
The mirrors show visitors as they believe themselves to be—and hold their price ledger. Break a mirror under Mirror Warrant to read its ledger safely.
Timer: Spotlight Heat [4] Encounter: The Mirror Reading (DV 4, Controlled)
💎 Mirror Warrant — Break a mirror without accruing Heat
🧙 The Wishwright's Tent
Velvet-law negotiation. Staff "assumes the difference" unless you have leverage. Divination finds missing clauses but attracts Livaea-touched attendants.
Timer: Carnival Appetite [8] Encounter: Negotiating with the Wishwright (DV 4, Controlled)
💎 Stage Permit — One lawful interruption of a live act
📖 The Ledger Wagon Heist
The carnival's traveling records office. Prices are written as negative space on vellum. Copying a ledger grants leverage to unwind a price—but the reader temporarily loses a related social grace.
Timer: Carnival Appetite [8] Encounter: The Ledger Heist (DV 5, Desperate)

🎵 Act III — The Calliope Heart

Tier III 1 Session

A public performance powers the engine. Break one pillar—Rhythm, Mask Index, or Ticket Chain—to starve it or retool it. Choose an ending: Expose & Regulate, Starve the Engine, or Divert the Appetite.

🎶 The Performance
The calliope plays. The engine feeds on the crowd's desire. The three pillars are vulnerable: Rhythm (crowd cadence), Mask Index (who plays which role), and Ticket Chain (consent tokens).
Encounter: Break the Rhythm (DV 5, Desperate) Encounter: Scramble the Mask Index (DV 5, Desperate) Encounter: Break the Ticket Chain (DV 5, Desperate)
💎 Calliope Key — Retune the engine's rhythm for a beat
✅ Ending: Expose & Regulate
The carnival stays—but all wishes require informed, spoken terms. Unraveling clears. Livaea's devotees take interest in you.
Encounter: The Regulation (DV 4, Controlled)
💥 Ending: Starve the Engine
Break a pillar. The tents collapse. Debts become letters and must be hand-delivered back to their owners.
Encounter: The Collapse (DV 4, Controlled)
🌀 Ending: Divert the Appetite
Offer a greater, voluntary group vow. The carnival leaves on a promise and returns when it matures. The debt is deferred—but not forgotten.
Encounter: The Vow (DV 5, Controlled)

⚙️ Special Mechanics

🎯 The Desire Tax
Every fulfilled wish costs something the wisher didn't know they valued. As more wishes accrue, the Desire Index rises and prices escalate.
Taxable Intangibles: Private memory, unconscious skill, social grace, local luck, shared custom, legal right, relationship texture, place-name, future possibility, invisible boundary.
Countermeasures: Informed consent (reduce Magnitude by 1), Substitution (block Appetite), Audited wish (use Witness Seal, –1 DV vs staff).
💎 Diamonds (Codified Leverage)
Witness Seal: Compels staff to name the exact price aloud.
Stage Permit: One lawful interruption of a live act.
Bellmark of Honest Trade: Shields from predatory refinances (–1 DV for term disputes).
Mirror Warrant: Break a mirror without accruing Heat.
Calliope Key: Retune the engine's rhythm for a beat (+1 Position vs engine hazards).
🎭 SB Spend Menus

⚔️ Bestiary

Livaea-Touched Attendant — TL 3, Armor 0, Harm 2, Harm Levels: 3 (standard). Humanoid (carnival).
Staff who have worked the Carnival long enough to start agreeing with it. They are unfailingly courteous, and the courtesy is the problem: every offer they make is real, and every one of them costs.
SB Spends: 1 — An Attentive Offer: a PC is offered exactly the small thing they want; accepting ticks Carnival Appetite +1. 2 — You Signed For This: the attendant produces a ticket stub, a receipt, a signature — the party is in the wrong and everyone nearby agrees. 3 — The Crowd Closes: the party is separated by bodies, cheerfully, and cannot regroup for an exchange.
Weakness: They cannot break the Carnival's own rules. Cite a Diamond the party holds and the attendant must stand aside, visibly irritated for the first time.
The Carnival Itself — TL 7, Harm Levels: None (puzzle). Place.
When Carnival Appetite [8] fills, this is what the grand act is. It is not a creature and cannot be attacked; it is a venue that has decided it is hungry, and every attendant, tent-flap and ticket is a part of it.
SB Spends: 2 — The Show Must Go On: a PC is drawn onto a stage and must perform or forfeit something. 3 — Rewrite the Bill: a location the party has already cleared is different when they return to it. 4 — Take the House: the exits are gone until the appetite is answered.
Resolution: It is closed, not killed. Present the Diamonds the party has earned as a complete bill of account — the Carnival honours its own paperwork above everything, including its hunger — or give it a performance so good it is satisfied, which requires someone to give up something they actually wanted.
