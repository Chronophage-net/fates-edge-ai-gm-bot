🏔️ The Grumbling Vault

Tier II 1 Session Dungeon Delve Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

Deep beneath the Aelerian foothills, a sealed chamber holds the Keystone of Unmaking — a fist-sized chunk of obsidian that can destabilize any ward within a mile. The Aeler locked it away centuries ago, but the wards have grown senile. They tick and pulse like a failing heart. Your goal: retrieve the keystone for a bitter old drunk who claims it's owed to him. By his beard, you'll earn every coin.

"You think you know stone? I've drunk ale older than your clan. The Iron Avengers could have held this vault with a teaspoon and a bad attitude. Now? Now you need runes and sparkles. Pathetic." — Durin Stoneshield, Iron Avenger

Reading an encounter badge. Each badge names a DV and a Position. It does not name a dice pool, and that is deliberate: the pool is Attribute + Skill, chosen from how the player says they are going about it. Two characters can attempt the same encounter with different pools and both be right. Where an approach is obviously the intended one, the badge says so; where it does not, ask what they are doing and build the pool from the answer.

🏔️ The Grumbling Vault

Tier II 1 Session

Three chambers. One grumpy dwarf. A timer that ticks with every spell. Let's do this.

🚪 Chamber 1: The Sealed Door
A massive bronze door, etched with Aeler runes that pulse faintly. No handle. No keyhole. Durin belches. The door hums with ancient power — and it's hungry for a reaction.
Timer: Ward Resonance [8] Timer: Durin's Debt [4] Encounter: The Door (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Ask Durin (DV 3, Dominant)
⚔️ Chamber 2: The Hall of Sundered Oaths
A long hall lined with suits of animated Aeler armor — three Iron Wraiths. They do not move until a spell is cast or a loud noise is made. They are resistant to magic, but vulnerable to being sundered.
Timer: Ward Resonance [8] Encounter: Iron Wraiths (DV 4, Controlled)
Iron Wraith TL 5 Harm Levels: 8 (advanced) Armor 2 Harm 3
Resistant to magic (-1 Harm). Sunderable: melee attacks can reduce armor. Death burst: deals 1 Fatigue to all in Near.
💎 Chamber 3: The Heart of Silence
A domed chamber. The Keystone of Unmaking floats above a black altar. Four pillars pulse with light. The Ward Resonance Timer is visible as a glowing ring around the keystone. If the timer fills, the Ward-Primus rises.
Timer: Ward Resonance [8] Encounter: Stealth Retrieval (DV 5, Controlled) Encounter: Ward-Primus (DV 5, Desperate)
Ward-Primus TL 8 Harm Levels: 8 per phase Armor 3 Harm 6
Immune to magic. Absorb Magic: negates a spell and heals 2 Harm. Weakness: mundane weapons deal normal Harm.

👤 Key NPCs

Durin Stoneshield
Iron Avenger (Retired, Drunk)
🎯 Get the keystone to settle an old debt — or just to have something to complain about.
Secret: He was never an Iron Avenger. He was a cook. But he tells a good story.

📍 Locations

The Sealed Door — A massive bronze door etched with Aeler runes that pulse faintly. No handle. No keyhole.
The Hall of Sundered Oaths — A long hall lined with suits of animated Aeler armor. The air is thick with the memory of broken promises.
The Heart of Silence — A domed chamber where the Keystone of Unmaking floats above a black altar. The walls pulse with stored energy.

🏛️ Factions

The Aeler Vault Guardians — Protect the keystone at all costs, even if their wards are senile. The guardians are remnants of a long-dead order. They don't negotiate — they just enforce.

⏱️ Campaign Timers

⚡ Ward Resonance 0/8
Each spell, rite, or magical action ticks this up. When full, the Ward-Primus awakens.
🍺 Durin's Debt 0/4
Each time you rely on Durin's advice, tick this. When full, he demands his 'trinket' — a rusty belt buckle.
