🩸 The Hollowed Hunger

Tier IV-V 3-4 Sessions Sequel Branching Hook Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

Months after the Hollowed Plague, the region is scarred and the debt is unpaid. Depending on how the party ended the plague, one of two crises now consumes the frontier: a tide of organized, hungering ghouls (the Worn Tide) spilling out of the desert under banners bearing Malachai's ship-sigil, or silver-eyed children who do not age and ask only one question — "What do you owe?" — dispensing clemency or blood-payment as they see fit (the Hollowed Hunger). The party is the only group who knows both factions by name. They must return to the threshold where it all went wrong and choose, again, what kind of mercy the world deserves.

"I thought the Needle was mercy. Now I wonder if mercy is just another word for control." — Sister Vorna, Winter of the Cord
⚠️ Safety and Consent at the Table

This adventure includes mass suffering, famine, undeath, body horror, and difficult moral choices with no clean resolution. It directly follows The Hollowed Plague and assumes the party has witnessed the Turned Hollowed already. Before play, establish Lines (hard boundaries — no sexual violence, no harm to children, no graphic torture), Veils (soft boundaries — describe the Hollowed's hunger obliquely rather than in detail), and confirm an X-Card is available to any player at any time.

Reading an encounter badge. Each badge names a DV and a Position. It does not name a dice pool, and that is deliberate: the pool is Attribute + Skill, chosen from how the player says they are going about it. Two characters can attempt the same encounter with different pools and both be right. Where an approach is obviously the intended one, the badge says so; where it does not, ask what they are doing and build the pool from the answer.

🏜️ Act I — The Scarred Land

Tier IV-V Branch Point

Months have passed. The Daughters are fractured, Malachai is more active than ever, and the party is known — as saviours, as liberators, or as those who failed to choose. This act branches hard at the Hook: which path the table plays is determined by how The Hollowed Plague ended, not by GM fiat.

🔀 The Hook — Two Roads From One Ending
Determine the branch from table history. Cord Path → the Worn Tide begins: Malachai used the party's intrusion into Zahk'Kozahn as a catalyst to tear open a rift, and organized ghouls now spill from the desert. Malachai Path → the Hollowed Hunger begins: freed Hollowed wander the caravan routes, silver-eyed and ageless, judging every debt they witness. Catastrophe (did nothing) → skip to the Appendix C epilogue instead of the full three acts.
Timer: Worn Tide [10] Timer: Hollowed Hunger [10] Encounter: The Messenger (DV 3, Controlled) Encounter: The Silver-Eyed Child (DV 3, Controlled)
🔍 Investigation — The Rift or the Hunger
The party gathers scene seeds along their branch: a village where every inhabitant has been judged; a fortress overrun by Worn under Malachai's ship-sigil; a Daughters' hospice quietly converted into a shrine to the Chained Angel. All roads trace to a single threshold.
Encounter: Trace the Source (DV 4, Controlled)
🤝 Social — The Faction's Plea
Sister Vorna offers a fragile alliance: healing, shelter, and loyal Hollowed scouts, but no help in a fight. If the party seeks the Hollowed's aid instead, Ellia or Collector Prime will help — for a price that is always social, never coin: a witnessed promise, a spared enemy, a surrendered memory.
Encounter: Negotiate with Sister Vorna (DV 4, Dominant) Encounter: Negotiate with Ellia/Collector Prime (DV 5, Controlled)
⚔️ Combat — The First Incursion
A tutorial encounter establishing the new threat's texture. Worn Ghouls attack in disturbing, disciplined order; Hungry Hollowed circle a settlement asking questions before they act. Neither should feel like a normal monster fight.
Encounter: Worn Ghouls, 1d4+2 (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Hungry Hollowed, 1d3+1 (DV 4, Controlled)

🔄 Act II — The Fork Inverted

The party must travel to the broken threshold and seal it. This act mirrors the original adventure's fork, inverted. Only one path is run per table.

Path A — The Worn Tide
🏕️ Step 1 — The Worn Siege Line
The approach to Zahk'Kozahn is blocked by a warband encampment. Infiltrate using captured Worn robes (requires a prior kill), or fight through directly.
Timer: Worn Morale [8] Encounter: Infiltrate the Siege Line (DV 5, Desperate) Encounter: Worn Captains, 1d3 (DV 5, Controlled)
Worn Captain TL 6 Harm Levels: 8 (advanced) Armor 2 Harm 4
Ichor Burst (2 SB): area Harm 2 in Near. Command Worn (3 SB): summon 2 Worn. Unnatural Vitality (1 SB): regenerate 1 Harm/round. Weakness: decapitation (DV 5 called shot ends regeneration).
⛪ Step 2 — The Cathedral Under Siege
Malachai has broken one arm free of the Aetherium chain. She cannot leave, but she directs the Worn and a handful of Turned Hollowed who still serve her. The party must fight through to reach the rift.
Timer: Worn Morale [8] Encounter: Worn Ghouls, 1d6+2 (DV 5, Desperate)
💠 Step 3 — Sealing the Rift
The rift bleeds ichor and Worn continuously. A Thread of the Cord, woven in through a Ritual Sealing, can close it: three successes (Arcana, Lore, or Presence) before two failures. Each failure triggers a Wave of Worn.
Timer: Ritual Sealing [3, 2-failure cap] Encounter: Ritual Sealing (DV 5, Desperate)
Path B — The Hollowed Hunger
🚪 Step 1 — The Liminal Descent
The House Without Doors is guarded now by Hollowed who never turned, and by those who have. The threshold trials are inverted: the party must prove they respect the Hollowed's new freedom, not their loyalty to the Cord.
Timer: House Trust [6] Encounter: Trial of the Witness (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Trial of the Unbroken Cord (DV 5, Dominant)
👑 Step 2 — The High Mother's Judgment
The High Mother lays out the choice with no compromise: mend the Cord and the Hollowed return to silence; cut it and she dies while they go free, hungering forever. This is a decision scene — roleplayed, not rolled.
No Roll — Decision Scene
⚖️ Step 3 — The Final Choice
Mend: the Hollowed lose sentience and return to silent witnessing; the High Mother survives, weakened; the party gains Cord's Gratitude — but some who tasted freedom will turn again. Cut: the High Mother dies, the House collapses, and the Hollowed become a new, lawless people; the party gains Hollowed Covenant — but the Daughters scatter for good.
No Roll — Consequence Resolves

🕯️ Act III — The Debt That Remains

An epilogue act. No combat here — its job is to let consequences land and seed future adventures.

🏔️ Epilogue — Worn Tide Aftermath
The Worn are pushed back to the desert and cut off from reinforcement. Malachai remains chained, but her one free arm reaches further than before. The Aeler holds honor the party and offer a permanent mountain hold. Sister Vorna, reluctant and grieving, becomes the new High Mother.
🏘️ Epilogue — Hollowed Hunger Aftermath
The Hollowed establish silent villages where no one ages and no one forgets. Some seek them out for judgment; others hunt them out of fear. The party is remembered either as the ones who gave freedom, or the ones who tried to take it back.
📖 Reference — Catastrophe Branch (Appendix C)
If the Cord broke outright, run this shorter branch instead of the full three acts: the Hollowed watch every birth, death, wedding, and treaty without attacking. The party must gather the Cord's scattered fragments and choose to re-weave it, destroy it forever, or broker a Covenant (very high DV, exceptional roleplay required). Bittersweet and tragic by design — do not pad it with combat.

🐉 Bestiary

Worn TL 3 undead

A ghoul risen from the desert's dead, no longer mindless — organized into warbands under officers who wear the stripped robes of Daughters as trophies. They shuffle in ranks, jaws working, waiting for an order.

Body 3 · Endurance 3 · Melee 2 · Armor 1 · Harm Levels: 3 (standard), Harm 2

Ichor Hunger (1 SB): feeds on a nearby corpse or downed PC, heals 2 Harm. Desperate Lunge (2 SB): once bloodied, gains an extra action this exchange.

Weakness: fire. A Worn hit by fire cannot use Ichor Hunger for the rest of the scene.

Worn Captain TL 4 undead

A Worn who kept enough of its old rank to command. It wears a Daughter's gray robe, stitched shut over the wounds that killed it, and carries whatever weapon it died holding.

Body 4 · Endurance 4 · Melee 4 · Armor 2 · Harm Levels: 3 (standard)

Ichor Burst (2 SB): area Harm 3 in Near, DV 4 halves. Command Worn (3 SB): summons 2 Worn. Unnatural Vitality (1 SB): regenerates 1 Harm/round.

Weakness: decapitation. A DV 5 called shot ends regeneration permanently.

Hungry Hollowed TL 3 fae-touched

A freed Hollowed whose sight has curdled into appetite. Silver wire still traces their eye sockets, but where the First Free merely witness, the Hungry judge — and collect.

Wits 3 · Presence 3 · Sway 3 · Harm Levels: 3 (standard)

Silver Gaze (1 SB): target answers truthfully or takes Harm 2 (Mental). Witness Recall (2 SB): reveals a witnessed secret about the target, aloud.

Weakness: cannot cross a threshold of running water.

Hungry Hollowed Judge TL 4 fae-touched

The pack-leader among the Hungry — the one whose hunger has gone furthest toward becoming law. It genuinely believes it is being fair, which is the most dangerous thing about it.

Wits 4 · Presence 4 · Sway 4 · Harm Levels: 3 (standard)

Silver Gaze (1 SB). Debt Collection (2 SB): target loses 1 Boon or takes Harm 3. Witness Summon (2 SB): calls a nearby Hungry Hollowed within one exchange.

Weakness: cannot harm anyone who has publicly confessed a debt to it.

Collector Prime TL 5 undead

Once a Silent One who chose Malachai's curse rather than remain a witness. It serves as liaison between the Worn and Malachai, speaking in flat, echoing tones that carry no breath behind them.

Body 4 · Wits 4 · Presence 5 · Armor 2 · Harm Levels: 8 (advanced), Harm 3

The Well Is Dry (2 SB): nullifies one Asset or String the target would call on this scene. Command the Line (3 SB): rallies 1d3 Worn immediately. Echo of the Thread (2 SB): target tests Spirit + Insight DV 4 or is shaken (-1 die to next roll).

Weakness: a Thread of the Cord, held and named aloud, forces it to answer one direct question truthfully before it can act again.

📍 Locations

The Aeler Frontier Garrison — A stone hold on the desert's edge, its walls scorched, besieged under banners bearing Malachai's ship-sigil.
The Converted Hospice — A Daughters' hospice quietly rearranged into a shrine to the Chained Angel. Candles burn around an empty chain-shaped altar.
The Cathedral at Zahk'Kozahn — Malachai's prison, one arm now free. The rift she tore open bleeds ichor and Worn into the nave.
The House Without Doors — The threshold where the Hollowed were unmade, guarded now by Hollowed who never turned and by those who have.
The Old Caravan Route Villages — Settlements along roads older than the Cord, visited by silver-eyed children who wait, patiently, for an honest answer.

🏛️ Factions

The Daughters of the Cord — Fractured allies. Sister Vorna's remnant will help the party, but cannot fight for them; the Cord itself is unstable.
The Worn — Hostile. Organized in a way that should not be possible for mindless ghouls, which is itself the horror.
The Freed Hollowed — Ambiguous. Some, led by Ellia, want restraint. Others, the Hungry, have let judgment curdle into appetite.
Malachai's Faction — Enemy. Collector Prime serves as its patient, unsettling voice, consolidating the Worn and any loyal Turned Hollowed.

🗒️ GM Notes & Secrets

Structure

A direct sequel with a branching hook set by the prior adventure's ending: Cord Path → Worn Tide (Path A, siege-and-rescue, back to Zahk'Kozahn); Malachai Path → Hollowed Hunger (Path B, threshold-trial-and-judgment, back to the House Without Doors); Catastrophe → redirect to the shorter Appendix C epilogue. Only one branch is run per table. Debts throughout are social and narrative — promises, witnessed oaths, surrendered memories, spared enemies — never coin or ledger language.

Act I Pacing

Commit to the branch immediately once table history is known — do not let players hedge between paths. Keep the investigation brisk; its job is to point at the return-to-threshold location, not to be a mystery in its own right. Sister Vorna's plea is the emotional anchor: grieving and bitter, not a quest-dispenser. The First Incursion is a tutorial — let the wrongness of organized ghouls or judging children land before the numbers matter.

Act II Pacing

Path A is a hard-timer siege-and-ritual structure — play it as escalating pressure toward a violent climax. Path B is a near-diceless trial-and-judgment structure — play it as a slow tightening of an impossible choice, culminating in a no-roll decision scene the table must actually debate. Do not treat Path B's lack of dice as lesser; it should take at least as long at the table as Path A.

Act III Pacing

No combat, no new dice. Its function is to let consequences land and seed future adventures (Rift Shard asset, Cord's Gratitude or Hollowed Covenant strings). Narrate as a montage; ask each player what their PC does in the weeks after and let that shape the epilogue.

NPC Secrets

Sister Vorna has quietly stopped believing the Needle was ever mercy; she has not said this to another Daughter. Ellia fears she is becoming a Judge rather than a witness — let this surface obliquely. Collector Prime chose its curse willingly and does not regret it; reveal only if directly, respectfully asked. Malachai's one free arm already lets her reach the Worn's minds directly, meaning Path A's resolution is only a partial victory — let this surface through continued Worn coordination after the fact, not as an announcement. The High Mother has known for years the Cord could not hold; her calm is dignity, not defeat.

Forbidden Early Revelations
  • Malachai's free arm reaching the Worn's minds — surfaces through play, never announced
  • Collector Prime's willing, unregretted choice — only on direct, respectful question
  • The High Mother's acceptance of the Cord's end — emerges through demeanor, not pre-stated
  • That some Mended Hollowed will eventually turn again — a future-adventure seed, not a warning
  • The exact wording of the High Mother's ultimatum — gesture at the stakes; don't pre-script the choice
Tactical Hints

Play Worn discipline as horror, not numbers — a single Worn standing at attention for an order that never comes is worse than a horde. Never let "What do you owe?" be rhetorical — make the player actually answer, briefly, at the table. Keep Ritual Sealing urgent through description (rising ichor, shaking ground) even without an explicit countdown timer. Resist adding a roll to the no-roll decision scenes — the tension is the table's deliberation, not the dice.
