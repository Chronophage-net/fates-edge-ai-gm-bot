🌿 The Hazel Root

Tier II–III 4 Sessions Folk Horror / Investigation Author: Nicholas A. Gasper Saga: Chained Angel Archives

A year after his encounter with Baron Asselin, Lerris Fenwood has returned to the University of Thepyrgos, hollowed and haunted. The memory of his mother's eyes has been stolen — a price exacted by the Ways Between. He is convinced that the Pathweaver, a mender of liminal wounds, can restore what he has lost. But the Pathweaver does not work for free. To earn her aid, Lerris must cross the Thornwood, bargain with the Hazel Queen, and survive the Huntsman's hunt. The party is drawn into the ancient war between the Alder King and the Hazel Queen, and the fragile peace that keeps the old powers at bay is about to shatter.

"The Alder King takes. The Hazel Queen bargains. The Huntsman follows. The Pathweaver forgets. In the old woods, you are never the hunter. You are only prey that has not yet been named." — Lerris Fenwood, Journal of the Hollowed

🌱 Premise & Tone

The Hook: Lerris Fenwood — the hollowed scholar — has returned to the University of Thepyrgos. He is not the same man who left. His mother's eyes are a void in his memory, a price exacted by the Ways Between. He is desperate, reckless, and willing to risk anything to restore what he has lost. He has heard of the Pathweaver, a mender of liminal wounds, who walks where the old powers have torn the world. He needs the party's help to find her — and to survive the war between the Alder King and the Hazel Queen.

This adventure bridges the Chained Angel Archives and the folk horror of the old powers. Lerris is a man hollowed by his encounter with Baron Asselin, carrying a lead-lined box and a gray thread that ties him to the Pathweaver. The party must navigate the ancient conflict between the Alder King (the root that strangles) and the Hazel Queen (the cunning hedge), while the Huntsman hunts them from the shadows.

Theme: Memory & Identity
What are you without your memories? Lerris has lost his mother's eyes — and with them, a piece of himself. The old powers offer to restore what was taken — but the price is always more than you expect.
Theme: The Cost of Knowledge
Every bargain in the Thornwood and the Hedge comes with a price. Lerris's quest is a journey into the heart of folk horror — where the land is sentient, the rituals have teeth, and children are currency.
🌿 The Old Powers: The Alder King and the Hazel Queen have been warring for centuries — not openly, but through proxies, curses, and the slow corruption of each other's domains. The Pathweaver mends what they break. The Huntsman hunts those who disturb the balance. Lerris is caught in the middle, and the Hollow watches.

📜 Act I: The Hollowed Scholar

Tier II 1 Session

Lerris Fenwood has returned to the University, but he is not the same man who left. The party is drawn into his quest for the Pathweaver — and into the ancient war between the old powers.

📓 The Hollowed Scholar
The party finds Lerris in the Archivolt. He is pale, hollow-eyed, and clutching a lead-lined box. He speaks in fragments, his mind scarred by the journey through the Ways Between. He offers a bargain: help him find the Pathweaver, and he will share what he learned about Malachai's curse.
Timer: Hollow Attention [6] Timer: Lerris's Desperation [8] Encounter: Lerris's Plea (Presence + Insight, DV 3)
Clean:You earn Lerris's trust. He shares the contents of the lead-lined box: Sister Aris's confession, a fragment of Malachai's bargain, and a single gray thread. He also reveals that the Pathweaver owes him a favor — he mended a threshold for her in his youth. Partial:Lerris trusts you, but he is holding back. He reveals Sister Aris's confession but not the gray thread. The GM gains 1 SB. Miss:Lerris's paranoia gets the better of him. He refuses to share anything. You must earn his trust through action.
🌿 Whisper: "The ink is still wet. The ninth page is blank. The Hollow reads over your shoulder. Lerris has seen the edge of the world — and it saw him back."
🧵 The First Thread
Lerris reveals that the Pathweaver's favor is a single gray thread, tied to a crossroads shrine near the Thornwood. He has not used it because he fears the price. The party must decide: help him find the Pathweaver, or warn him against the bargain.
Timer: Lerris's Desperation [8] Timer: Hollow Attention [6] Encounter: The Crossroads Shrine (Wits + Lore, DV 4)
Clean:You reach the shrine without incident. The gray thread is tied to a hazel branch. Lerris touches it, and the Pathweaver appears — but she is not happy to see him. She demands a new favor: retrieve a hazel nut from the Hazel Queen's Hedge. Partial:The Pathweaver appears, but she is guarded. She demands a price for her aid: a memory from each party member. Miss:The Pathweaver refuses to appear. Lerris is devastated. Tick Lerris's Desperation +2.

🌀 Act II: The War of Root and Hedge

Tier II–III 1–2 Sessions

The party enters the Thornwood, seeking the Hazel Queen's Hedge. They are caught between the Alder King's dark patience and the Hazel Queen's cunning bargains. The Huntsman begins to hunt them.

🌲 The Thornwood
The forest is wrong. The trees are too close, the light too dim, the roots too eager. Navigation is impossible. The party must find the Hedge before the Thornwood claims them.
Timer: Thornwood Timer [10] Timer: The Hunt Timer [12] Encounter: Navigate the Thornwood (Wits + Survival, DV 4) Encounter: Briar Wights (Cap 3, Harm 2)
Clean:You find a path through the forest. Tick Thornwood Timer -2. You see a clearing ahead — the Hedge. Partial:You make progress, but the forest shifts behind you. Tick Thornwood Timer +1. The path is still ahead. Miss:You are lost. Tick Thornwood Timer +2. The forest closes in.
🌿 Whisper: "The roots have learned your names. You cannot leave the Thornwood without a sacrifice — a memory, a drop of blood, a promise. The Alder King is patient. He will wait."
🌰 The Hazel Queen's Hedge
The Hedge is a living labyrinth of thorn and hazel. The Hazel Queen is waiting. She offers Lerris a choice: she can restore his mother's eyes — but only if he brings her a thorn from the Alder King's crown.
Timer: Hedge Debt Timer [8] Timer: Lerris's Desperation [8] Timer: The Hunt Timer [12] Encounter: The Hazel Queen's Bargain (Presence + Sway, DV 5)
Clean:You negotiate a fair bargain: the Hazel Queen will restore Lerris's memory in exchange for a single thorn from the Alder King's crown. She also warns you that the Huntsman is close — and that the Alder King will not give up his thorn willingly. Partial:The Queen agrees, but she demands a memory from each party member as collateral. Mark 1 Obligation to the Hazel Queen. Miss:The Queen refuses to bargain. She demands Lerris stay with her as a hostage. Tick Hedge Debt +3.

🌲 Act III: The Huntsman's Hunt

Tier II–III 1 Session

The party must retrieve the Alder King's thorn. But the King is not willing to part with it. The Huntsman is closing in. The Pathweaver offers a third path — but her aid comes at a price.

👑 The Alder King's Throne
The party finds the Alder King's throne — a massive alder tree at the heart of the Thornwood. The King is waiting. He offers a bargain: a thorn from his crown, in exchange for a seed from the Hazel Queen's Hedge.
Timer: Thornwood Timer [10] Timer: The Hunt Timer [12] Timer: Hedge Debt Timer [8] Encounter: The Alder King's Bargain (Presence + Resolve, DV 6)
Clean:You negotiate a fair bargain: the Alder King will give you a thorn in exchange for a seed from the Hazel Queen's Hedge. He also warns you that the Huntsman is already here — and that he is not the only one hunting you. Partial:The King agrees, but he demands a service: bring him the head of the Huntsman. The party must choose between the King and the Hunt. Miss:The King refuses to bargain. He attacks. Combat begins.
🌿 Whisper: "The Alder King does not ask. He offers. Each bargain is a trap that feels like salvation. He will give you what you want — and take what you need."
🧵 The Pathweaver's Third Path
The Pathweaver appears at the crossroads between the Thornwood and the Hedge. She offers a third path: she will restore Lerris's memory herself — but only if he agrees to become her student, learning to mend thresholds for the rest of his life.
Timer: Lerris's Desperation [8] Timer: Hollow Attention [6] Encounter: The Pathweaver's Offer (Presence + Insight, DV 5)
Clean:Lerris accepts the Pathweaver's bargain. She restores his memory — but he is now her apprentice. He will leave the University and walk the ways between. The party gains a permanent ally in the Pathweaver. Partial:Lerris accepts, but he is changed. He gains the Condition 'Hollowed by the Path' (-1 die to social rolls with ordinary people). Miss:Lerris refuses. The Pathweaver vanishes. Tick Lerris's Desperation +2. The party must find another way.

⚖️ Act IV: The Final Reckoning

Tier III 1 Session

The Huntsman has cornered the party. The Alder King and Hazel Queen watch from the edges. Lerris must make a final choice: accept the Pathweaver's bargain, take the thorn, or find another path.

🐺 The Huntsman's Court
The Huntsman corners the party at a crossroads. He offers no bargain — only a choice: one member of the party stays behind as a sacrifice, or the entire party faces his hounds. Lerris is willing to sacrifice himself.
Timer: The Hunt Timer [12] Timer: Hollow Attention [6] Encounter: The Huntsman's Choice (Presence + Resolve, DV 6)
Clean:You find the Huntsman's true name (hidden in a Briar Wight's memory) and speak it. The Huntsman retreats. The Hunt ends. Tick The Hunt Timer to 0. Partial:You speak the name, but it is corrupted. The Huntsman is weakened, but not banished. Tick The Hunt Timer -4. Miss:You fail to speak the name. The Huntsman attacks. Combat begins.
🌿 Whisper: "The Huntsman's true name is carved into the Alder King's throne. To speak it is to end the hunt — but to speak it is to owe the King a debt."
🏆 Resolution Paths
Lerris's memory is restored — but the war between the Alder King and the Hazel Queen continues. The party must choose how to end the conflict, or how to survive it.
The Pathweaver's Peace
The Pathweaver weaves a truce between the King and Queen. The war is frozen for a generation. Lerris becomes the Pathweaver's apprentice.
Reward: 15–18 XP. Permanent ally in the Pathweaver.
The Huntsman's Service
The Huntsman is bound to the party's service. He will hunt the party's enemies — but the price is a memory each season.
Reward: 18–20 XP. The Huntsman as an ally.
The Hollow's Bargain
The party offers a memory to the Hollow to settle the war. The King and Queen are both diminished. The party is marked by the Hollow.
Reward: 12–15 XP. The party is marked by the Hollow.
The War Continues
The party fails to broker peace. The war escalates. The ninth threshold opens. Something ancient enters.
Reward: 10–12 XP. The party must flee or face the Ninth.

📍 Key Locations

The Archivolt, University of Thepyrgos — The main hall of the Library of Keys. Witness's Archivists hold court. Lerris has been hiding here, reading forbidden texts and waiting for the party.
The Crossroads Shrine — A place where the Pathweaver appears. The gray thread is tied to a hazel branch. The air smells of honey and old promises. The thread is warm.
The Thornwood — A shifting forest that appears where alders grow in circles. The Alder King's domain. Paths lead nowhere. Roots reach for the unwary. The ground is always damp.
The Hazel Queen's Hedge — A living labyrinth of thorn and hazel. The Hazel Queen's domain. Each path offers a choice. Each choice has a price. The nuts are warm.
The Alder King's Throne — A massive alder tree at the heart of the Thornwood. The King's crown is woven from living branches. His throne is a nest of roots. The Huntsman's true name is carved into the bark.
The Crossroads of the War — Where the Thornwood meets the Hedge. The Pathweaver appears here. The ground is a tapestry of root and thorn. The air smells of old war and older bargains.

🏛️ Factions

Lerris Fenwood — Wants to restore his mother's memory. Desperate, reckless, and willing to pay any price. He is the party's client and moral compass. He carries a lead-lined box and a gray thread.
The Alder King — Wants to expand his domain and win the war against the Hazel Queen. He is patient, territorial, and cruel. He will bargain — but the price is always a trap.
The Hazel Queen — Wants to collect debts and win the war against the Alder King. She is cunning, quick, and exact. She will bargain — but the price is always a memory.
The Huntsman — Wants to hunt those who disturb the old powers. He serves neither king nor queen. He is patient, silent, and deadly. He will not bargain — only hunt.
The Pathweaver — Wants to restore balance and mend what the war has broken. She is neutral, ancient, and exacting. She will offer a third path — but the price is always a change.
The Hollow — Not a faction, but a pressure. It watches the war. It waits for the threshold to open. It is patient, hungry, and eternal. Lerris is marked by the Hollow.

⏱️ Campaign Timers

🌲 Thornwood Timer 0/10
How deeply the forest has claimed the party. Advances when the party enters the Thornwood, rests, or harms a creature of the wood. At 9–10, the Alder King manifests and offers a bargain — or a trap.
🌰 Hedge Debt Timer 0/8
How much the party owes the Hazel Queen. Advances when the party accepts her aid, breaks a promise, or fails to pay a debt. At 7–8, the Queen appears and demands payment.
🐺 The Hunt Timer 0/12
How close the Huntsman is to catching the party. Advances each day of travel, plus additional ticks for leaving traces or using magic. When full, the Huntsman corners the party and offers no bargain — only a choice.
🌀 Threshold Fracture 0/14
How close the war is to breaking reality. Advances when the party takes sides, breaks a bargain, or kills a fae creature. When full, the ninth threshold opens and something enters — something even the King and Queen fear.
👁️ Hollow Attention 0/6
How much the Hollow has noticed Lerris's quest. Advances when he speaks of Malachai, opens the lead-lined box, or lingers in thresholds. When full, the Hollow Bride appears — offering a bargain, demanding a price.
🔥 Lerris's Desperation 0/8
How close Lerris is to making a reckless bargain. Advances when the party fails to help him, when he is reminded of his loss, or when the Hollow speaks. When full, Lerris will accept any bargain — even a deadly one.

🐉 Bestiary

The Alder King
Tier V Fae Harm 6
The spirit of the dark, wet wood. Patient, territorial, and cruel. His domain is the Thornwood. He does not hate you — but he will not let you go.
SB Spends: 2 – Root Grasp (target is Grappled); 3 – The King's Bargain (target must accept a geas or take Harm 3); 4 – Thornwood Manifestation (all navigation rolls are Desperate for the scene).
Weakness: Running water. The Alder King cannot cross a stream or river that flows toward the sea.
The Hazel Queen
Tier V Fae Harm 5
The hag of crossroads and hedgerows. She offers exactly what you need — for a price you will not understand until the hazel nuts have fallen thrice.
SB Spends: 1 – Hedge Debt (target marks 1 Obligation); 2 – Crossroads Choice (target must choose between two bad outcomes); 3 – The Queen's Price (target loses a memory).
Weakness: Salt. The Hazel Queen cannot cross a line of salt. She will not bargain with someone who carries a handful of salt.
The Huntsman
Tier IV Fae Harm 4
The Silent Tracker. He serves neither king nor queen — he serves the hunt itself. His hounds are the ghosts of wolves. His true name is carved into the Alder King's throne.
SB Spends: 2 – The Hunt Begins (advance The Hunt Timer by 2); 3 – Hound Attack (a ghost-wolf attacks, Cap 3); 4 – The Huntsman's Choice (target must sacrifice a memory or be caught).
Weakness: A true name. If the party discovers the Huntsman's true name and speaks it, the Hunt ends.
The Pathweaver
Tier IV Fae Harm 3
The mender of liminal wounds. She walks where the Alder King and Hazel Queen have torn the world. She weaves gray thread and mends thresholds. Her aid is never free.
SB Spends: 1 – Unknot the Way (open a locked door or sealed promise); 2 – Weave the Lost Path (reveal a hidden route); 3 – Mend the Threshold (permanently repair a broken threshold — but cost a memory).
Weakness: She cannot refuse a true request made at a crossroads — but she will exact a price for her aid.
Briar Wight
Tier III Undead Harm 3
A traveler who died in the Thornwood, their body claimed by the Alder King's roots. They move slowly, their limbs wrapped in thorn and bark. They remember who they were — and they are angry.
SB Spends: 1 – Thorn Grasp (target is Grappled and takes Harm 1); 2 – Root Burst (all within Close take Harm 1).
Weakness: Fire. A Briar Wight exposed to fire must test Spirit (DV 3) or flee.
