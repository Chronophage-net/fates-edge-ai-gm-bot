🧩 The Ninth Proof

Tier II–III 3–4 Sessions Cosmic Horror / Investigation Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

Beneath the University of Thepyrgos, a forgotten game has been rediscovered. Kon'reh — the Ninth Rim — is not merely a strategy game; it is a proof, a prayer, and a doorway. A secret society of scholars believes it holds the key to rewriting reality. The Ninth's Coterie wants to weaponize it. And the Witness's Archivists are desperate to seal it away before the game's final move is played.

"Count eight. Leave the ninth to open." — winter leaf from Dhahara

🎯 Premise & Tone

The Hook: A scholar named Lerris Fenwood has gone missing from the University of Thepyrgos. His brother Markus — a junior archivist — has found a cryptic notebook in his quarters, filled with diagrams of a game called Kon'reh and references to "the Ninth Rim," "the Salt-Pressed Leaves," and "a door that opens when the board is complete."

The party is hired to find Lerris. What they discover is a conspiracy that spans centuries: a secret society of scholars who believe that Kon'reh — the ancient game of the steppe — encodes a mathematical proof that can reshape reality itself. The game's final move is called "The Ninth Proof." If completed, it could unmake the boundaries between what is and what could be.

Theme: The Price of Knowledge
Every revelation in the University comes with a cost. The Ninth Proof promises transcendence — but only if you are willing to sacrifice what you know.
Theme: The Hollow's Attention
The Ninth's Coterie is not the only force watching the game. The Hollow itself has taken notice. Every move brings the party closer to a door that should not be opened.
🔮 The Ninth Rim: In the game of Kon'reh, the ninth rim is the outermost ring of the board. To cross it is to leave the game — and enter something else entirely. The scholars who study it whisper that the ninth rim is not a rule, but a door.

📜 Act I: The Game Awakens

Tier II 1 Session

The party investigates Lerris's disappearance, discovering the Kon'reh notebook and the secret society of scholars who play it. They must navigate the University's politics while the game begins to reveal its true nature.

📓 The Missing Scholar
Markus Fenwood, Lerris's brother, is desperate. He has found a notebook in Lerris's quarters filled with cryptic diagrams. The last entry reads: "The Ninth Rim opens at the salt-pressed hour. I will count to eight and leave the ninth for the door."
Timer: Lerris's Fate [8] Timer: Hollow Attention [6] Encounter: The Notebook (Wits + Lore, DV 3)
Clean:You decode the notebook's basic structure — Kon'reh is a game of encirclement and escape, but the diagrams suggest something more: a proof that rewrites the rules of the board. Partial:You understand the game's surface, but the deeper meaning eludes you. you gain 1 Boon; the GM has whatever Story Beats your 1s gave them, to use on a "Ninth Rim" complication. Miss:You misread a diagram; the Hollow notices. Tick Hollow Attention +1 and you gain 2 Boons.
🔮 Whisper: "The ink is still wet. The ninth page is blank. The Hollow reads over your shoulder."
♟️ The Game Begins
The party is approached by a scholar from the Ninth's Coterie — a secret society of the Ninth's adherents. They offer a deal: help them complete the proof, and they will reveal Lerris's location. But their offer is a trap — the proof requires a sacrifice.
Timer: Ninth's Progress [8] Timer: Witness's Vigil [6] Encounter: The Coterie's Offer (Presence + Sway, DV 4)
Clean:You extract information without committing. You learn that Lerris is being held in the Sunken Archive — a forbidden section of the University sealed by the Witness. Partial:You learn Lerris's location, but you owe the Coterie a favor. Mark 1 Obligation to the Ninth. Miss:You accept a poisoned deal. Tick Ninth's Progress +2. The Coterie now has leverage over you.
🔐 The Sunken Archive
The archive is a labyrinth of forbidden texts, sealed by the Witness's Archivists. It is here that Lerris is being held — and where the Kon'reh board is being assembled. The party must infiltrate the archive and rescue Lerris before the proof is completed.
Timer: Lerris's Fate [8] Timer: Witness's Vigil [6] Encounter: Infiltrate the Archive (Stealth + Deception, DV 4)
Clean:You reach Lerris. He is alive, but changed — he has been playing Kon'reh for days, and the game has begun to affect his mind. He mutters: "The ninth rim is not a rule. It is a door. The Hollow answered." Partial:You find Lerris, but the Archivists are alerted. You must escape through the Whispering Gallery (see Clubs). Miss:You are caught. The Archivists detain you. Tick Witness's Vigil +2.

🌀 Act II: The Ninth Rim

Tier II–III 1–2 Sessions

Lerris is free, but the game is already in motion. The party must learn the true nature of Kon'reh, navigate the war between the Witness and the Ninth, and decide which faction — if any — to support.

🧠 Lerris's Revelation
Lerris explains: Kon'reh is not just a game. It is a proof — a mathematical demonstration that reality is a board. The ninth rim is the edge of the board. Those who cross it can rewrite the rules of existence. The proof is almost complete. The Coterie wants to complete it. The Witness wants to seal it.
Timer: Ninth's Progress [8] Timer: Witness's Vigil [6] Encounter: The Proof Explained (Wits + Lore, DV 5)
🔮 Whisper: "The ninth word is never spoken. The ninth path is hidden. The ninth name is erased from history. The proof is the ninth move — and once it is played, the board will never be the same."
⚖️ The Archivists' Demand
The Witness's Archivists arrive. They demand that the party hand over the Kon'reh notebook and any fragments of the proof. They offer protection in exchange. But their protection is a cage — they will seal the knowledge away, and Lerris with it.
Timer: Witness's Vigil [6] Encounter: The Archivists' Offer (Presence + Sway, DV 4)
Clean:You convince the Archivists to share information without surrendering the notebook. You learn that the proof was originally discovered by Aqyl the Elder — and that he sealed it for a reason. Partial:You negotiate a compromise: you will share the proof, but you keep the original. The Archivists agree — but they will watch you closely. Miss:The Archivists seize the notebook. Tick Witness's Vigil +2. You must recover it.
♟️ The Game Board
The Coterie has assembled the Kon'reh board in a hidden chamber beneath the University. The board is not a game — it is a prayer. Each piece is a name. Each move is a memory. The final move — the Ninth Proof — will be played at the next salt-pressed hour.
Timer: Ninth's Progress [8] Timer: Hollow Attention [6] Encounter: The Board (Wits + Lore, DV 5)
🔮 Whisper: "The board is a threshold. The pieces are names. The moves are memories. The ninth move is a door — and on the other side, something is waiting."

🔮 Act III: The Final Proof

Tier III 1 Session

The salt-pressed hour approaches. The party must decide the fate of the Ninth Proof: complete it, seal it, or destroy it. The Hollow is watching. The Ninth is waiting. And the game is almost over.

⌛ The Salt-Pressed Hour
The Coterie is gathered in the chamber beneath the University. The board is complete. The salt ring glows. The Hollow watches. The Ninth Proof is one move away. The party must act now — or the door will open.
Timer: Ninth's Progress [8] Timer: Witness's Vigil [6] Timer: Hollow Attention [6] Encounter: The Final Choice (Presence + Insight, DV 5)
🔮 Whisper: "Count eight. Leave the ninth to open. The door is waiting. The Hollow is patient. The game is almost over."
🏆 Resolution Paths
The party has three options — each with lasting consequences.
Complete the Proof
The Ninth Proof is played. Reality rewrites itself. The party gains immense knowledge — but the Hollow gains a foothold in the world. Lerris is lost to the game. The Ninth's Coterie is victorious.
Reward: 18–20 XP. A permanent connection to the Ninth. The Hollow's Attention follows the party forever.
Seal the Proof
The party aligns with the Witness's Archivists. The proof is sealed away — but the knowledge is lost. The Coterie is destroyed, but the Ninth's influence lingers. The University is safe, but the secret is buried.
Reward: 12–15 XP. A permanent connection to the Witness. The Archivists owe you a favor.
Destroy the Proof
The party destroys the board and the notebook. The proof is lost forever. The Ninth's Coterie is shattered. But the Hollow remembers — and it will seek another way.
Reward: 10–12 XP. The party is marked by the Ninth as enemies. The Hollow's Attention is reduced — but not gone.
The Ninth's Bargain
The party negotiates with the Ninth itself. The proof is not completed, but the Ninth is appeased. The party gains a boon — but the Hollow gains a name.
Reward: 15–18 XP. A Ninth's Boon (once per session, reroll any roll). The party is forever marked by the Hollow.

📍 Key Locations

Lerris's Study — A cramped room in the University's north wing. The walls are covered in Kon'reh diagrams. The salt ring on the desk is still damp.
The Sunken Archive — A sealed section of the Library of Keys. The books here are forbidden. The Archivists guard them with their lives — and their silence.
The Salt-Pressed Chamber — A hidden room beneath the University. The floor is a Kon'reh board. The walls are lined with names. The air tastes of salt and old promises.
The Archivolt — The main hall of the Library of Keys. The Witness's Archivists hold court here. The books whisper truths — and lies.
The Ninth Rim — The outermost edge of the Kon'reh board. To cross it is to leave the game. The party may be forced to cross — or to prevent others from doing so.

🏛️ Factions

Markus Fenwood — Wants to save his brother. Desperate, loyal, and willing to bend the rules. He is the party's first contact and a moral compass.
The Ninth's Coterie — Wants to complete the proof. They believe the Ninth will bring liberation — but the Ninth only brings forgetting. Their leader is the Voice of the Ninth.
The Witness's Archivists — Wants to seal the proof. They believe knowledge must be preserved and protected. They are willing to sacrifice Lerris to keep the proof hidden.
Aqyl, Son of Aqyl — Wants the truth to be known — but only at the right time. He is the keeper of the Salt-Pressed Leaves. He knows the proof's true nature.
The Hollow — Not a faction, but a pressure. It watches the proof. It waits for the door to open. It is patient, hungry, and eternal.

⏱️ Campaign Timers

🔮 Lerris's Fate 0/8
How close Lerris is to being lost to the game. Advances when the party delays, when the Coterie completes a move, or when the party ignores a clue. When full, Lerris becomes the final piece — the Ninth Proof is played.
🌀 Ninth's Progress 0/8
How close the Ninth's Coterie is to completing the proof. Advances when the party accepts Coterie bargains, fails to stop a move, or ignores a warning. When full, the Ninth Proof is complete — the door opens.
📜 Witness's Vigil 0/6
How close the Witness's Archivists are to seizing the proof. Advances when the party aids the Coterie, avoids the Archivists, or breaks archive rules. When full, the Archivists raid the party's safehouse and seize the notebook.
👁️ Hollow Attention 0/6
How much the Hollow has noticed the party's involvement. Advances when the party speaks names, completes moves, or lingers in sealed chambers. When full, the Hollow Bride appears — offering a bargain, demanding a price.

🐉 Bestiary

The Hollow Bride
TL 6 Harm Levels: 8 (advanced) Outsider Harm 4
A manifestation of the Ninth, appearing when the proof is near completion. She offers bargains that seem fair — but she collects her price in names, memories, and promises. Her voice is honey and razors.

SB Spends: 1 – Paradox (target must reroll a success); 2 – Bargain of Forgetting (target gains +1 die for the scene but loses a memory); 3 – The Ninth's Authority (the target must obey one command or take Harm 2).

🧩 Puzzles

The Kon'reh notebook contains several puzzles that the party must solve to progress. These puzzles are diegetic — they appear as diagrams, marginalia, and encoded notes. Use them as scene hooks or as obstacles to accessing the proof.

📐 Puzzle 1: The Candle Ledger
A page in the notebook shows a row of candles. Each candle is labeled with a number. The numbers are in base-5. The candles that are lit form a sequence. The sequence is a count. The count is a door.
Hint (H1): Count the lit candles in order. The numbers are not in base-10.
Hint (H2): The sequence is 2, 3, 1, 2, 3 — translate to base-5: 23123.
Hint (H3): Translate 23123 (base-5) to a letter using the Quinary Code Table.
🗺️ Puzzle 2: The Map at Seventeen
A city map shows gatehouses and sanctums. Rotated 17°, the map overlays the Kon'reh board. Five seal marks on the map's rim land on five specific squares. Each seal encodes a digit.
Hint (H1): Rotate the map 17° clockwise. Align the sanctums with the gatehouses.
Hint (H2): The seal marks are: △, •, ∥, ∗, □. Use the Quinary Code Table.
📜 Puzzle 3: The Veil Page
A deposition leaf has black redaction bars. The first line is deliberately blank. The next five lines have redaction bars. Count the bars on each line — 0 to 4 — to form a 5-digit base-5 code.
Hint (H1): Skip the blank line. Count the bars on the next five lines.
Hint (H2): Each continuous black rectangle counts as one bar, regardless of length.
🧩 Quinary Code Table (Simplified)
The notebook uses base-5 codes to encode letters. Each 5-digit code maps to a letter:
00000 → A, 00001 → B, 00002 → C, 00003 → D, 00004 → E,
00010 → F, 00011 → G, 00012 → H, 00013 → I, 00014 → J,
00020 → K, 00021 → L, 00022 → M, 00023 → N, 00024 → O,
00030 → P, 00031 → Q, 00032 → R, 00033 → S, 00034 → T,
00040 → U, 00041 → V, 00042 → W, 00043 → X, 00044 → Y,
00100 → Z
Example: 23123 (base-5) → 2×625 + 3×125 + 1×25 + 2×5 + 3 = 1250 + 375 + 25 + 10 + 3 = 1663. The 1663rd letter of the sequence is... but in practice, use the table or ask the GM.
