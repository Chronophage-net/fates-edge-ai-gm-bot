⚒️ The Dwarven Debt

Tier II-III 4-5 Sessions Honor & Oath Underground Exploration Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

Decades ago, one of the party owes House Khaz-Vurim an oath sworn in good faith and never properly honored — a promise entangled with a ceremonial hammer, the Forgefather's Voice, pledged as surety and left unclaimed when the original bargain went unfinished. Now the house is faltering, its name losing weight among the other holds, and Vault-Queen Thora Stonebeard has decided the old promise must finally be kept. To honor it, the party must descend into the Spine Underway, where something ancient has been waiting far longer than any dwarf for that promise to be settled — and where "debt," the party will learn, was never really about the hammer at all.

"A dwarf's word outlives his forge. If Khaz-Vurim's word means nothing, then neither does the stone we stand on." — Clan-Speaker Thrain Ironhold
Table note: "Debt" in this adventure is always social currency — an oath, a kinship, a piece of reputation owed. It is never a ledger entry, a sum, or an accounting figure. Keep every timer, toll, and consequence framed as trust and standing, not bookkeeping.

🔥 Act III — The Deep Stirrings

The party reaches the Forgefather's Crypt. This isn't a story about a missing object — it's about a promise between the dwarves and something ancient, long before Khaz-Vurim's bargain ever existed.

⚱️ The Forgefather's Crypt
A sacred resting place, warded and locked by ceremony rather than mechanism.
Timer: Ward Standing [6] Approach with Proper Rite (DV 5, Desperate) Consult Durin's Secret (DV 4, Controlled)
🗣️ The Stone-Singer Speaks
The Stone-Singer wants to know whether the old kinship the bargain represented is still honored — or has become just another transaction to be settled and forgotten.
Speak with the Stone-Singer (DV 5, Desperate)
The Stone-Singer TL 8 Resistance 3 Harm Levels: None (puzzle)
Prefers patience over confrontation. Stone-Song Whisper (1 SB). Resonant Pressure (2 SB). The Old Bargain Remembered (3 SB).
Resolution: It has no body to cut and does not understand the question. It is answered, not defeated: prove that the old kinship the bargain represented is still honoured — in deed, where it can feel the difference through the stone — or say plainly that it is not, and live with what it does about that.

The party returns carrying the full truth — not just a hammer, but the real shape of what's owed, to whom, and why.

🗒️ GM Notes

Debt language guardrail: Never describe House Standing, the bargain, or any resolution in ledger or accounting terms. Always frame as trust, kinship, reputation, and honor.
Act I: Establish dwarven honor culture as something to respect, not merely navigate. Thora's dignity should come through even in decline.
Act II: Vary the tunnels' texture. Stone-Song Resonance should build a sense of being watched, not hunted — the Deep Stirrings are patient, not predatory.
Act III: The reveal that the hammer represents an older kinship should land with weight but without melodrama — this is a shared, sympathetic complication, not a shocking betrayal.
Act IV: Reward players who've built genuine relationships. The best ending leaves all three parties — Khaz-Vurim, the Stone-Singer, and the party — with their dignity intact.
