🐍 The Serpent's Coil

Tier II 3 Sessions Sword & Sorcery Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

By Crom! A Tale of Sorcery, Serpents, and Stubborn Heroes. The city of Midh Ahkaz runs with fear, though nobody will admit it. A serpent cult has grown fat on whispered promises, and the Governor's Palace now houses something that doesn't cast a proper shadow. The heroes—whatever combination of muscle, blade, and questionable morals brought them together—are about to stumble into the middle of it.

⚔️ The Heroes Are Not the Sharpest Swords They solve problems with axes, miss clues until they trip over them, and tend to believe the first explanation offered. Play them with affection. A heavy brow and a trusting nature are their own kind of courage.

Reading an encounter badge. Each badge names a DV and a Position. It does not name a dice pool, and that is deliberate: the pool is Attribute + Skill, chosen from how the player says they are going about it. Two characters can attempt the same encounter with different pools and both be right. Where an approach is obviously the intended one, the badge says so; where it does not, ask what they are doing and build the pool from the answer.

🐍 The Serpent Spawn Ambush
Nightmares made flesh—grotesque fusions of human and serpent. Their mouths drip venom that sizzles on stone. They climb walls. They have no sense of humor.
Timer: Cult Ascendancy [6] Encounter: Ambush (DV 5, Desperate)
👩 Yara's Revelation
Yara is beautiful but twisted—patches of iridescent scales, vertical pupils, a smile that's too wide. She is not a victim. She's a volunteer. She thinks becoming a snake-person is "cool." The heroes must explain why this is a bad idea. With small words.
Encounter: The Revelation (DV 4, Controlled)

🌅 Resolution
The aftermath tastes of copper, sweat, and victory—or bitter defeat. The city will remember the heroes, whether they want it to or not. The bards will sing. The songs will be mostly accurate.

The Black Goat Tavern — Not as scary as it smells. The barmaid is a cultist, but she's just in it for the dental plan.

⚙️ Special Mechanics

🐍 Serpent's Favor (GM Eyes Only)
A secret track the GM keeps for each character. Starts at 0. Increases when Isoka's influence touches them. As Favor rises, the character experiences subtle luck—coincidences, good timing, strange intuitions. They think they're becoming a legendary hero. They are, but also they're becoming something else.
0–3: Nothing yet.
4–7: +1 die once per scene from "instinct."
8–12: +1 die to all rolls involving "serpent intuition."
13+: +2 dice, but civilized folk start giving you strange looks.
🔄 Corruption Score
Track separately from Favor. This is the actual transformation. Players see it. Starts at 0.
0–5: None. Just a bit of dry skin.
6–10: Gain Serpent's Senses (+1 die to Insight in dark/underground places).
11–15: Gain Scaled Skin (+1 Armor). People start edging away from you.
16+: Gain Cosmic Insight (once/session, ask a question about cosmic threats). You also hiss your "s"s.
⚔️ Resolution Paths
Greater Good: Embrace transformation on your own terms. +5 Corruption. New cosmic allies/enemies.
Heroic Stand: Smash the altar, rally the guards. +2 Corruption. The city builds statues.
Tactical Retreat: Blow up the ichor pools and escape with Yara. +1 Corruption. Sequel hooks abound.
Power Bargain: Negotiate a limited transformation. +3 Corruption. Isoka finds you interesting.
Sacrifice Play: One hero holds the portal closed. +10 Corruption for the fallen. The others gain a Legendary Bond.

⚔️ Bestiary

Palace Guard — TL 3, Armor 1, Harm 2, Harm Levels: 3 (standard). Humanoid.
Midh Ahkaz's household soldiery: competent, bored, and paid on time. Most of them have not noticed what is happening under the palace, and would rather not.
SB Spends: 1 — Raise the Cry: a second pair arrives next exchange. 2 — Cordon: one exit is closed and held. 3 — Pin the Blade: a PC is grappled against a wall, Desperate until they break free.
Weakness: Authority. A convincing order from someone who sounds like they outrank the sergeant works better than a sword, and everyone in the room knows it.
Serpent Spawn — TL 4, Armor 1, Harm 3, Harm Levels: 3 (standard). Beast (serpent-touched).
What the cult's half-finished ascensions leave behind. Fast, silent, and still wearing enough of a face to make killing it feel like something.
SB Spends: 1 — Drop from Above: it attacks from a ceiling or gallery; that attack is Desperate to defend against. 2 — Venom: Harm 1 that recurs each exchange until treated (Wits + Craft, DV 3). 3 — The Nest Answers: two more come, and they came from a direction nobody was watching.
Weakness: It remembers being a person. Speak the name it had, if anyone knows it, and it will not attack that character for the rest of the scene.
Thaliss, First of the Coil — TL 6, Armor 2, Harm 4, Harm Levels: 8 (advanced). Humanoid (ascended).
The cult's high priest, three-quarters through a transformation he chose and now cannot stop. He is still articulate. That is the horror of him.
SB Spends: 1 — Coiling Word: a PC who answers him takes −1 die until they stop listening. 2 — Call the Coil: two Serpent Spawn arrive. 3 — Shed: he sloughs a layer and clears one Harm Level, and what falls away keeps moving.
Weakness: He wants a witness more than he wants to win. A character who genuinely engages with what he is becoming can hold him in conversation while others act.
Isoka, Angel of Serpents — TL 9, Harm 6, Harm Levels: None (puzzle). Outsider.
She is not summoned so much as attended to. She finds her own devotees exhausting and says so. She has no interest in a fight and will not have one.
SB Spends: 2 — The Coil Tightens: every PC's Corruption ticks 1. 3 — An Honest Offer: she names precisely what one PC wants and precisely what it costs, and she is not lying. 4 — Withdraw Her Attention: the ritual chamber begins to close, whoever is still in it.
Resolution: She cannot be fought and does not need to be banished. Break the ritual's terms — the cult's claim on her attention is contractual, and contracts have clauses. Undo the claim and she leaves, mildly relieved. Fulfil it and she also leaves, and the world is worse.
