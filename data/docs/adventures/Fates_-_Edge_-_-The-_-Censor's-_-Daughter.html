The Censor's Daughter

Tier III 1-2 sessions Exorcism Investigation Faith Debt Cosmic Horror Redemption Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

In Ecktoria, a fourteen-year-old girl speaks in forgotten tongues and the Inquisition has set a date for her burning. Her father, a man who has never believed in anything except the ledger, refuses to help. But his books hold a secret: the demon's name is a debt, and debts can be paid. The party has three days to uncover the truth and perform an exorcism that will either save a child or doom her soul forever.

The Three Calls: An Exorcism Deduction Mini-Game

The demon's true name is its first lie—the clause it hid, the violence it used as a key, the moment the host said 'yes' to something they should have refused. Players must uncover three traits through research and investigation, then speak the name during a three-stage exorcism ritual.

Invisible Contract. The demon's first lie was a hidden clause in a contract. The Censor signed a loan he could not read. Found by: Audit the hole with The Witness, or find the original contract in the archive.

Ledger Debt. The demon's name is recorded in the Censor's private ledger—a debt that was sold to a collector who died. Found by: Investigate the Censor's ledgers, or interrogate Fabius.

Impossible Debt. The interest on the loan could never be paid. The debt is eternal—and that is the demon's nature. Found by: Cross-reference loan records, or discover that the collector died before the debt could be settled.

First Call. “I name you [TRUE NAME]. You are seen. You are witnessed.” — DV 5, 1 trait(s). Success: Proceed to Second Call. Failure: Demon gains 1 thread; +1 segment.

Second Call. “I name you [TRUE NAME]. You are bound. You are contained.” — DV 5, 2 trait(s). Success: Proceed to Third Call. Failure: Host gains 1 FATIGUE; demon breaks eye contact; +1 segment.

Third Call. “I name you [TRUE NAME]. You are unmade.” — DV 5, 3 trait(s). Success: Demon mirror shatters; shards fly inward. Failure: Demon possesses exorcist mirror.

📝 Running It

This adventure is adapted from Saikou Ira's case files. The exorcism mini-game is the mechanical centerpiece—players must conduct research, gather witnesses, and perform the Three Calls while managing the Possession Depth timer. The adventure supports multiple resolution paths: expose the Censor, perform a secret exorcism, or break the girl out. Each path has its own costs and consequences. The themes of debt, obligation, and witness are central—the party must decide what they are willing to pay to save a child. Designed for a single session of 4–6 hours, but can be extended into a mini-campaign by expanding the investigation phase.

The First Day: The Weight of the Ledger

The party arrives in Ecktoria to find a city on edge. The Inquisition has set a date for the burning. The Censor's daughter is held in a silver-lined cell. And her father—a man who has never believed in anything except the ledger—refuses to help. The party must investigate the Censor's books, find the demon's true name, and prepare for the exorcism. They have three days.

The Censor's House

Fabius Valerius sits in his study, surrounded by ledgers. He is a man who has spent his life enforcing contracts; he cannot admit that he broke one. His daughter, Livia, has been possessed for three years—ever since the night his wife died. The loan he took to pay for her medicine was sold to a collector, then to a broker, then to something that has forgotten it was ever human. The demon is the debt, given will. And it has been living in his ledger for three years.

The Second Day: The Three Calls

The party has the demon's true name. Now they must perform the exorcism—a ritual in three mirrors, witnessed by three souls. The circle must be drawn, the mirrors placed, and the Three Calls spoken. If they fail, the demon will possess the exorcist's mirror and walk free. If they succeed, the girl will be saved—but the exorcist will pay a price.

The Circle of Salt and Iron

The exorcism must be performed in a space that can be sealed. The prison cell is the obvious choice—but the Inquisition watches. The party must draw the circle, place the mirrors, and invoke the Sentinel. They have one chance.

The Aftermath: The Price of Witness

The girl is saved—but the exorcist paid a price. A memory is gone, and the debt never closes. Fabius must face the consequences of his actions. The Inquisition watches. The chain-lanterns of the Quiet Seal know what happened. And the demon's shards are scattered, waiting to be found.

The Censor's Confession

Fabius Valerius confesses to loansharking. The Inquisition arrests him. Livia is freed, but she never speaks again—not because she cannot, but because she has nothing left to say. The Chain-Lanterns of the Quiet Seal offer the party a choice: help them expose the Inquisition's corruption, or disappear before the Flame-Wardens arrive.
