🎭 Blood and Silk: The Complete Saga

Tier I–III 3–5 Sessions Saga Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

The full Blood and Silk campaign arc — from exile in Silkstrand to the Bronze Gate. Follow a group of exiles as they carve a place for themselves in the broken world of Fate's Edge. This saga includes The Velvet War of Silkstrand (prequel), Blood and Silk (Millhaven), Beyond Millhaven (the Valvano Ford), and The Brass Gate (Castra Ferrum).

📜 Prologue: The Velvet War of Silkstrand

Tier I 1 Session

The fall of the Silk Coin, a principled thieves' guild. The player characters make choices that cost them their place in Silkstrand. Their Legacy Tokens carry into the main adventure.

🕯️ The Velvet Court's Gambit
Madam Serafine has declared the Silk Coin a threat to her operations. The guildhall is surrounded. Old Kes urges the characters to flee while they still can. The players must decide: stand and fight for a doomed cause, or run and carry the Silk Coin's legacy into exile.
Timer: The Court's Net [4] Encounter: The Last Job (DV 3, Desperate)
🏙️ The City You Left Behind
Silkstrand is a city of bridges and dyewater. The Matron rules from a palazzo of velvet and iron. Your names no longer appear on guild rolls. Your friends have forgotten your faces — or claim they have.
🛤️ The Road to Exile
You have been erased. The road ahead is uncertain. Millhaven is just a name on a map — a chance to start over where no one knows your story. Or so you believe.

⚔️ Act I: Blood and Silk — The Defense of Millhaven

Tier I 2 Sessions

The exiles arrive in Millhaven, a farming village threatened by the Ursillo Gang. They must earn the villagers' trust, defend the settlement, and decide whether to stand and fight or flee once more.

🏘️ Arrival at Millhaven
The party arrives at Millhaven to find a village under siege. Elder Thorne explains the Ursillo Gang's demands and the villagers' dwindling options. The party must decide: help the village or move on?
Timer: Village Safety [6] Timer: Ursillo Threat [8] Encounter: Meeting Elder Thorne (DV 3, Controlled)
⚡ The Ursillo Gang's Ultimatum
Garrick Ursillo and his men arrive to collect "protection money." The villagers have nothing left to give. The party must choose: pay the gang, fight them, or negotiate.
Timer: Ursillo Threat [8] Encounter: Confronting Garrick (DV 4, Controlled)
🗺️ The Strategic Crossroads
Elder Thorne and the party must decide on a primary strategy. Option A: Fortify and Hold — build defenses, train a militia. Option B: Strike First — take the fight to the Ursillo camp.
Encounter: The War Council (DV 3, Controlled)
⚔️ The Final Stand
Garrick Ursillo has gathered all his forces for one final assault on Millhaven. The battle will decide the village's fate. The Ursillo Morale Timer and Village Defense Timer will determine the outcome.
Timer: Village Defense [6] Timer: Ursillo Morale [8] Encounter: The Battle of Millhaven (DV 4, Desperate) Encounter: Confronting Garrick (DV 5, Controlled)
🌅 Aftermath: The Village Stands or Falls
The battle is over. If the village stands, the party has earned a home. If it falls, the survivors must decide where to go next. Tema the Vilikari merchant offers a path forward in either case.

🌉 Act II: Beyond Millhaven — The Ford of Three Promises

Tier II 2 Sessions

The party escorts a grain caravan to the Valvano Ford, where Ykrul herders, Aeler toll-keepers, and Vilikari merchants are locked in a standoff. They must mediate or risk all-out war.

🧑‍🌾 Sorian Tabb's Offer
The party meets Sorian Tabb, a Vilikari caravan master, at the Muddy Stilt. He offers honest work: escort a cargo of grain seed to the monastery of St. Arilan. The pay is modest. The real prize is a Letter of Introduction — a chance to leave Acasia behind.
Encounter: The Contract (DV 3, Controlled)
🏛️ Arrival at the Valvano Ford
The ford is closed. A silence furlong seals the east bank. A keystone clause locks the bridge. A Black Banner mercenary counts coin while three factions refuse to move. The party must break the deadlock.
Timer: Ford Standoff [8] Encounter: Reading the Scene (DV 3, Controlled)
🤝 The Three Promises
The party must meet each faction and uncover their hidden needs. The Ykrul demand honor restoration. The Aeler need a face-saving audit. The Vilikari need to hide a salt cache. The Black Banner needs payment.
Timer: Ford Standoff [8] Encounter: Khatun Sarnai (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Factor Voss (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Captain Rusk (DV 3, Controlled)
⚖️ The Mediation
The moot is held. The party must bring the factions to agreement. Every success cools the standoff; every failure advances the timer. If the timer fills, the standoff collapses into violence.
Timer: Ford Standoff [8] Encounter: The Moot (DV 5, Desperate)
🛤️ The Road Ahead
The ford is open. The party has earned a reputation as negotiators. Tema offers a share in her future profits. The road to the Bronze Gate lies ahead, carrying rumors of a cursed seal and a heist.

🏰 Act III: The Brass Gate — Heist, Curse, and the Fall of the Ninth Legion

Tier III 2 Sessions

The party is hired by Tema to steal the Iron Seal of Varus from Prefect Marcellus's tribunal. The seal can bind oaths with a wasting curse — and Marcellus intends to use it on the Ykrul. The party must navigate a heist, a curse, and the politics of Castra Ferrum.

📦 Tema's Warehouse — The Job
Tema offers a fortune in silver to retrieve the Iron Seal of Varus from the Prefect's tribunal. She wants to give it to Khatun Sarnai as a peace offering, opening the steppe trade route.
Timer: Heist Timer [6] Encounter: The Offer (DV 3, Controlled)
🔑 The Bronze Gate — A Tour of Factions
The party must navigate the politics of Castra Ferrum. Prefect Marcellus, Censor Cassia, Khatun Sarnai, Captain Rusk, and Quick Lena all have their own agendas.
Timer: Heist Timer [6] Timer: Watch Patrol [4] Timer: Cult of Varus [8] Encounter: Prefect Marcellus (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Censor Cassia (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Khatun Sarnai (DV 4, Controlled)
💎 The Tribunal Heist
The party must infiltrate the Prefect's tribunal and steal the Iron Seal of Varus. Quick Lena is already inside, working for Captain Rusk. The Watch Patrol timer advances on failed Stealth rolls.
Timer: Heist Timer [6] Timer: Watch Patrol [4] Encounter: Chamber One — The Armory (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Chamber Two — The Scriptorium (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Chamber Three — The Reliquary (DV 5, Desperate)
🐍 The Curse Unsealed
The seal is in the party's possession — but it carries the old curse. The party must decide how to break the curse: return it to the Ykrul, destroy it with fire, or bind it with a new oath.
Encounter: Return to the Ykrul (DV 4, Controlled) Encounter: Destroy with Fire (DV 6, Desperate) Encounter: Bind with a New Oath (DV 5, Controlled) Secret Fourth Way: The Ninth Clause (DV 5, Dominant)
🌅 The Gate Opens
At dawn, the portcullis rises. The Ykrul riders wait. The merchants line up with their carts. The party has earned a place in Fate's Edge.
Encounter: The Ending (DV 3, Dominant)

📍 Locations

Silkstrand — A city of bridges and canals where favours are measured in threads.
Millhaven — A small farming community in the Acasian marches.
The Old Imperial Bridge — A bridge with nine arches. The locals count only eight.
The Pale Causeway — The last high road that survives the spring thaws.
The Valvano Ford — A contested crossing where three factions are locked in a standoff.
Castra Ferrum — A walled city built atop a Ykrul burial ground.
The Bronze Gate — The only pass through the mountains for three weeks' march.
The Praetorium — The fortified hall where Prefect Marcellus holds his tribunal.
The Archive — A labyrinth of scrolls and curse tablets awaiting destruction.

🏛️ Factions

The Silk Coin — A principled thieves' guild that kept its word even when it cost everything. Now fallen.
The Velvet Court — Control Silkstrand's underworld through velvet gloves and iron fists.
The Ursillo Gang — Extract "protection money" from villages, grow their power in Acasia.
The Vilikari Traders — Open trade routes, profit from grain and goods.
The Ykrul Gray Ash Clan — Protect their people, restore clan honor, reopen trade.
The Aeler Guild — Maintain the toll rights and charter at Valvano Ford.
The Black Banner Mercenaries — Get paid, get out. Sell steel to whoever can pay.
The Ecktorian Tribunal — Enforce order, control the Bronze Gate, use the seal to force oaths.
