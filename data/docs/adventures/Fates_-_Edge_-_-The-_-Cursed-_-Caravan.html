🐪 The Cursed Caravan

Tier II 3 Sessions Folk Horror Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

A merchant caravan has been cursed while crossing the Pale Causeway. Supplies rot, animals die, and strange omens appear. The players must escort the caravan to safety while uncovering a conspiracy involving a powerful entity that feeds on suffering. Folk horror and survival with multiple moral resolutions.

"The Pale Causeway remembers every footstep. It also remembers every soul that never reached the other side." — Dusana of the Raven Road

⚔️ Act I: The Cursed Caravan

Tier II 1 Session

Players encounter the caravan and experience the curse's immediate effects. They must decide to help and begin investigating the source.

🛑 The Desperate Merchant
The party finds the caravan stalled on the road. Merchant Aldric Thorne offers a reward for help. The curse manifests as supplies rot and animals die.
Timer: Curse Escalation [8] Timer: Caravan Supplies [6] Encounter: First Signs (DV 3, Controlled)
Clean:You stabilize the immediate crisis and gain the merchant's trust. Partial:You save some supplies, but the curse shows its teeth. Miss:Animals die and supplies rot further. Tick Curse Escalation +1 and Caravan Supplies -1.
🧙 The Hedge-Witch's Warning
Hedge-Witch Mira Blackwood approaches in a tavern, warning of the dark force moving along the trade routes.
Encounter: Gaining Knowledge (DV 4, Controlled)
Clean:You learn that the curse is tied to a sealed chest and can be contained with folk rituals. Partial:You gain some information, but she demands a favor. Miss:She clams up, sensing you are not ready for the truth.

🌲 Act II: Into the Blackwood

Tier II 1 Session

The party seeks help from the hedge-witch and faces the growing influence of the Hungering Dark.

📦 The Chest Investigation
The party examines the mysterious sealed cargo item that is the source of the curse.
Timer: Curse Escalation [8] Timer: Hungering Dark's Strength [6] Encounter: Examining the Chest (DV 5, Desperate)
Clean:You discover the chest contains a bound entity and learn how to perform a containment ritual. Partial:You learn the nature of the curse but trigger a minor backlash. Tick Curse Escalation +1. Miss:The chest lashes out, causing Harm 1 to the party and advancing Hungering Dark's Strength +2.
🕯️ The Protective Ritual
Using the hedge-witch's knowledge, the party performs a protective ritual to resist the curse.
Encounter: Ritual of Warding (DV 4, Controlled)
Clean:You gain Folk Protection Tokens (salt, red thread, butter-left charm) and reduce the Curse Escalation by 1. Partial:The ritual works but consumes materials and leaves you exposed. Miss:The ritual fails, and the entity takes notice. Tick Hungering Dark's Strength +1.

⚡ Act III: The Cursed Crossroads

Tier II 1 Session

The final confrontation at the Cursed Crossroads, where the party must choose between containment, bargain, or destruction.

🌑 The Confrontation
The party faces the Hungering Dark's strongest influence at the crossroads.
Timer: Curse Escalation [8] Encounter: The Hungering Dark (DV 6, Desperate) Encounter: The Cursed Child (DV 6, Desperate)
Clean:You successfully contain the curse through ritual or destroy the entity. Resolution achieved. Partial:You strike a bargain with the entity, buying safety at a cost. Miss:The entity escapes, and the curse spreads further. The caravan is lost.

👤 Key NPCs

Merchant Aldric Thorne
Caravan Leader
🎯 Save his livelihood and family reputation
Knows the chest was given to him by a stranger who warned him not to open it.
Hedge-Witch Mira Blackwood
Local Supernatural Practitioner
🎯 Protect the community from supernatural threats
Knows more than she reveals; she has personal stake in the curse.
Margravine Xara Rothari
Regional Ruler
🎯 Maintain order and authority
She is aware of the curse but sees it as a tool to control the region.

📍 Key Locations

The Pale Causeway — The last high road that survives the spring thaws. Crossings are perilous, and the road seems to shift and change.
Blackwood Hollow — A charcoal burner's clearing with a hedge-witch's dwelling, protected by strong wards.
The Cursed Crossroads — Meeting point of multiple roads, a place of choice and consequence where the Hungering Dark's influence is strongest.

🏛️ Factions

The Merchant's Caravan — Survive and reach safety. The party is hired to protect them.
The Hungering Dark — Feed on suffering and break free of its prison. The party must confront or contain it.

⏱️ Campaign Timers

🌑 Curse Escalation 0/8
How quickly the curse worsens. Advances when the party fails to contain it or when the entity feeds.
📦 Caravan Supplies 0/6
Depletes as supplies rot. When empty, the caravan cannot continue.
🌀 Hungering Dark's Strength 0/6
Increases when the entity gains power through fear and suffering.

🐉 Bestiary

The Hungering Dark
Tier IV Outsider Harm 6
An ancient entity that feeds on suffering, despair, and life force. It spreads its curse through manipulation, emotion, and possession.
SB Spends: 1 – Whisper of Despair (target gains 1 Fatigue and must test Resolve or mark Dread); 2 – Grip of the Curse (all enemies in Near zone suffer -1 die to actions for one exchange); 3 – Manifest Hunger (deal Harm 2 to all in zone and advance Curse Escalation by 1).
Mistwraith
Tier III Undead Harm 3
Secrets made flesh; they feed on awareness and fear. Appear where the party hesitates or falters.
SB Spends: 1 – Ethereal Touch (target loses 1 Fatigue and gains Condition: Haunted).
Shadewalker
Tier III Undead Harm 4
Corpse-puppets bound in root and bone. Slow, relentless, territorial.
SB Spends: 1 – Relentless Advance (ignore one point of Fatigue and gain +1 die to next attack).
