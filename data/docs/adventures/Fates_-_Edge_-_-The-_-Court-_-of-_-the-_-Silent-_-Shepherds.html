🕊️ The Court of the Silent Shepherds

Tier III 2-3 Sessions Political Intrigue Social Combat Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

The Court of the Silent Shepherds — the gathering place where Aelinnel elders have settled disputes and guided their people for a thousand years — has fallen into an unnatural silence. Every elder stands frozen mid-gesture, mid-argument, mid-alliance, unable or unwilling to speak. One elder manages to scrawl a warning before going still herself: the first to speak will break the silence, but at a price. The party is summoned as mediators to find the source of the silence, break it without breaking the court, and discover why the Aelinnel's most sacred tradition chose this moment to stop.

"In the silence, every thought becomes a shout. In the court, every whisper is a command."

Reading an encounter badge. Each badge names a DV and a Position. It does not name a dice pool, and that is deliberate: the pool is Attribute + Skill, chosen from how the player says they are going about it. Two characters can attempt the same encounter with different pools and both be right. Where an approach is obviously the intended one, the badge says so; where it does not, ask what they are doing and build the pool from the answer.

🗣️ Act II — The Unspoken Price

Speaking against the silence exacts a toll — always social, never a ledger entry. The party must decide who speaks, when, and what they owe.

💬 Breaking the Silence
Any attempt to speak against the silence costs the speaker something real: a favor called in, a standing strained, a promise that follows them home.
Timer: Unspoken Price [8]
Speak Against the Silence — DV 4, Controlled
Clean: Silence yields. Court Tension improves +1. Toll owed is proportionate.
Partial: Silence cracks; speaker pays a toll, court remains volatile.
Miss: Attempt fails. Unspoken Price +1; minor toll owed regardless.
Unspoken Toll (narrative, never ledger language):
Minor: A debt of favor comes due early, a small confidence must be shared.
Moderate: A relationship is visibly strained; standing among your own people quietly dips.
Severe: A new, unwanted obligation of loyalty is incurred — it will follow the speaker past this adventure.

🗒️ GM Notes

Tone guardrail: The Unspoken Price toll must always read as social — a favor, a strained bond, an obligation — never as a number owed or a ledger balance.
Act I: Let the frozen court feel eerie, not puzzle-boxy. Reward players who actually look at individual elders.
Act II: The toll should be proportionate to how the silence was broken, never arbitrary punishment.
Act III: The High Shepherd's secret should land as tragic and understandable — a leader's fear of failing their people, not a mystery to interrogate.
Forbidden early revelations: Don't reveal the Shepherd entered willingly before Act III; don't reveal the Songweaver bypass before trust is earned; keep the Silent Watch's role ambiguous throughout.

⚔️ When the Court Fractures

This adventure has no monsters, and that is not an oversight — it is a room full of people who will not speak. But Court Tension reaching Fracturing promises violence, so here is what that violence is. It is never a set-piece battle; it is one bad moment between people who have known each other for two hundred years.

Aelinnel Elder (any of them) — TL 4, Armor 0, Harm 3, Harm Levels: 3 (standard). Small Folk.
Old, formal, and entirely out of practice at this. An elder who draws is humiliated before the first exchange and knows it. They fight badly, briefly, and never twice.
SB Spends: 1 — Said Aloud: something unforgivable is finally spoken; tick Unspoken Price +2. 2 — Take a Side: another elder commits, and the room divides. 3 — The First Blood: someone is hurt who was not part of this, and the court cannot pretend afterwards.
Weakness: Witness. An elder will stop the instant a PC makes it clear they are watching and will remember. Nobody here wants to be the one who started it.
The Silence Itself — TL 6, Harm Levels: None (puzzle). Condition.
What actually opposes the party. It has no body, holds no weapon, and wins every scene in which nobody says the true thing.
SB Spends: 1 — The Moment Passes: a PC who hesitated loses the opening. 2 — Everyone Agrees Not To: a truth spoken aloud is politely ignored, and the speaker takes −1 die socially for the scene. 3 — Calcify: tick Unspoken Price +2; what has gone unsaid this long starts to look permanent.
Resolution: Somebody says it, in front of everyone, and is not talked out of it. That is the whole adventure. Fracturing is what happens when the party runs out of time to do it gently.
