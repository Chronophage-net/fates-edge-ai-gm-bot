🌫️ Whispers in the Tunnels

Tier II–III 3 Sessions Cosmic Horror Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

Beneath Thepyrgos, the tunnels remember what the city above has forgotten. Routine maintenance on ancient Aelerian seals goes catastrophically wrong when the Deep Drake — an ancient psychic entity — stirs in the flooded depths. The PCs are trapped underground as reality warps, whispers multiply, and their own minds become battlegrounds for its freedom. Survive, or become part of its eternal song.

"Beneath Thepyrgos, the tunnels remember what the city above has forgotten." — Dusana of the Raven Road

Reading an encounter badge. Each badge names a DV and a Position. It does not name a dice pool, and that is deliberate: the pool is Attribute + Skill, chosen from how the player says they are going about it. Two characters can attempt the same encounter with different pools and both be right. Where an approach is obviously the intended one, the badge says so; where it does not, ask what they are doing and build the pool from the answer.

🚪 Session 1: Trapped in the Depths

Tier II 1 Session

The PCs are investigating seal failures when psychic screams echo through the stone. Floodgates slam shut, and black water rises. The Deep Drake's whispers begin. The party must survive the initial collapse and find shelter.

🌊 The Flood Escape
Water is rising fast. The tunnel is collapsing. The party must navigate to higher ground while carrying an injured Rennik. The water is unnaturally cold and seems to pull at limbs.
Timer: Environmental Doom [8] Timer: Deep Drake Awakening [10] Encounter: Escape the Flood (DV 3, Desperate)
👁️ The First Whisper
In the relative safety of a dry chamber, the party hears whispers — their own names, their private fears. The Deep Drake has noticed them. The whispers offer a way out — but the price is a memory.
Timer: Dread (Individual) [10] Timer: Deep Drake Awakening [10] Encounter: Resist the Whisper (DV 3, Controlled)
🔮 Whisper Example: "The water remembers your name. It remembers everyone who drowned here."
🧮 Rennik's Paranoia
Rennik begins muttering equations under his breath. He is convinced the numbers are 'wrong' — that the tunnel layout is shifting. He may be the first to break.
Timer: Dread (Individual) [10] Timer: Collective Sanity [10] Encounter: Calm Rennik (DV 4, Controlled)

🌀 Session 2: Descent into Madness

Tier II–III 1 Session

The party must navigate the Whispering Maze, bargain with ghostly merchants in the Bone Market, and learn the history of the Deep Drake from the Crying Workshop. Reality becomes unstable as the entity's influence grows.

🦴 The Bone Market
A haunted hall where ghostly merchants offer bargains that cost more than expected. Time flows strangely here. The party can trade memories for information — but every trade feeds the Deep Drake.
Timer: Deep Drake Awakening [10] Timer: Dread (Individual) [10] Encounter: The Bone Merchant's Bargain (DV 4, Controlled)
🔮 Whisper Example: "Your friends' thoughts are not their own. Can you tell which memories are real?"
🔥 The Crying Workshop
An ancient forge where the original binding stones were created. The forge weeps tears of dark oil, and the tools hum with malevolent energy. Here, the party can learn how to create new binding stones — or accidentally wake the Drake fully.
Timer: Deep Drake Awakening [10] Timer: Environmental Doom [8] Encounter: Study the Forge (DV 5, Desperate)
🌀 The Whispering Maze
The tunnels are now actively hostile — they shift when unobserved, doors lead to different times, and echoes whisper lies. The party must navigate to the Drowned Palace before the maze consumes them.
Timer: Deep Drake Awakening [10] Timer: Collective Sanity [10] Encounter: Navigate the Maze (DV 4, Desperate)
🔮 Whisper Example: "Sacrifice is the only path to salvation. What are you willing to give up?"

🏛️ Session 3: The Drowned Palace

Tier III 1 Session

The party reaches the heart of the Deep Drake's influence — an underwater throne room where reality is at its thinnest. The entity offers a final bargain: power, escape, or oblivion. The party must choose how to end the nightmare.

👑 The Throne of the Deep Drake
The water here is thick with memory. The throne pulses with a heartbeat that matches your own. The Deep Drake's presence is overwhelming — it offers to restore the dead, grant power, or simply let the party leave — if they pay the price.
Timer: Deep Drake Awakening [10] Timer: Dread (Individual) [10] Timer: Collective Sanity [10] Timer: Environmental Doom [8] Encounter: The Deep Drake (DV 5, Controlled) Encounter: The Final Choice (DV 5, Dominant)
🔮 Whisper Example: "You were meant to be more than human. Embrace what you were always meant to be."
The Deep Drake TL 9 Harm Levels: 8 per phase Eldritch Harm 6
Psychic entity — cannot be harmed by mundane weapons. Requires ritual or psychic combat to defeat. Abilities: Psychic Whispers, Reality Distortion, Memory Theft.

📍 Key Locations

Sealed Junction — Chest‑deep black water that absorbs light. The carved warnings shift when unobserved. The seal mechanism is cracked and leaking dark water.
Bone Market — A haunted hall where time flows inconsistently. Ghostly merchants offer bargains from stalls that span centuries.
Crying Workshop — An ancient forge that weeps dark oil. Diagrams of binding rituals cover the walls — some complete, others tantalisingly unfinished.
Whispering Maze — Corridors that shift when unobserved, doors that lead to different times, and echoes that whisper lies.
Drowned Palace — An underwater throne room where the water is thick with memory. The Deep Drake's presence is overwhelming.

🏛️ Factions

The Deep Drake — Break free from its ancient binding, consume the minds of all who enter its domain. The party's primary antagonist — it offers bargains, but every deal feeds it.
Tunnel Survivors — Escape the tunnels, resist the Drake's influence. Rennik and Mira are key allies — if they survive, they can guide the party to safety.

⏱️ Campaign Timers

🐉 Deep Drake Awakening 0/12
The entity's progression toward full manifestation. When full, the Drake partially manifests — Reality Collapse for 1 scene.
🌊 Environmental Doom 0/8
Structural integrity of the tunnel system. When full, the tunnels collapse — immediate escape or death.
🧠 Collective Sanity 0/10
Party‑wide mental stability. When full, shared hallucinations become real — all Insight rolls are Desperate.
😰 Dread (Individual) 0/10
Personal psychological deterioration. At 7+ Dread, physical transformation (Drakespawn) begins. Can be reduced by spending Boons.
